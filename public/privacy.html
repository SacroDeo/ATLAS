Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

ATLAS is a personal goal assistant that works through a Telegram bot (@AtlasGrowbot) and a web dashboard. This page explains, in plain words, what data ATLAS stores and why. ATLAS is run by an individual developer, not a company.

What we collect

  • Telegram basics — your Telegram ID, first name, and username, sent to us by Telegram when you message the bot or use Telegram login. We never see your phone number or password.
  • What you tell the bot — your goal, your daily-time preferences, your task check-ins, and chat messages you send to the bot. This is the whole point of the product: it needs your goal to plan your days.
  • Google sign-in (optional) — if you use "Continue with Google", Google shares your email address and account ID with us. We never see your Google password. We use this only to let you log in to the dashboard.
  • Progress data — tasks generated for you, which ones you completed or skipped, and your streak.

What we do NOT collect

  • No payment information (ATLAS is free).
  • No location data, contacts, or files.
  • No tracking cookies, ads, or analytics scripts on the website. The only cookie we set is your login session.

How your data is used

  • To generate your roadmap and daily tasks, we send your goal and task-related messages to AI providers (Groq and Google Gemini) for processing. They process it to produce the response and are not permitted to use free-tier API data to identify you personally.
  • To show your stats on the dashboard.
  • Never sold. Never shared with advertisers. No mailing lists.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database and processed on a Render server. Both are reputable cloud providers with encryption in transit and at rest.

Deleting your data

Send /reset to the bot to erase your profile, goal, roadmap, and tasks at any time. For complete removal of everything including your account row, message the bot and ask, or contact the operator on Telegram — it will be deleted manually within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above changes and significant changes will be announced through the bot.

Contact

Questions? Message @AtlasGrowbot on Telegram.
