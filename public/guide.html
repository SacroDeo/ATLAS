📖 The ATLAS Guide

Everything you can do with your AI personal goal assistant — in 5 minutes.

1. Getting started

  1. Open @AtlasGrowbot on Telegram and press Start
  2. Answer the onboarding questions — your goal, your current level, and when you want your tasks each morning (your local timezone)
  3. ATLAS builds your personal roadmap and starts coaching. That's it — you're live.

Have your own plan already? You can paste a custom roadmap instead and ATLAS will follow yours.

2. Your daily loop

  • Every morning, ATLAS sends the day's tasks, generated from your roadmap — at the hour you chose, in your timezone
  • Tap /start anytime to see today's tasks as ✅ tappable buttons — tap one to mark it complete
  • /today — how many missions remain today
  • /progress — today's completion + your current 🔥 streak
  • Complete tasks daily to grow the streak. Miss a day and ATLAS checks in on you — it notices when you go quiet.

3. Just talk to it — no commands needed

ATLAS understands natural language. Type what you want, like you'd text a friend:

Manage tasks

add a task to revise SQL joins add a task for tomorrow: prepare demo slides delete task 3 change task 2 to "read chapter 5 instead" show my tasks

Manage your goal

show my goal update my goal — I want to focus on backend now show my roadmap

Get support

I'm feeling stuck and unmotivated today explain what a REST API is

ATLAS remembers your history — your goals, struggles, and wins from past weeks shape how it coaches you. Sometimes it answers a question with a question: that's Socratic mode, checking you actually understood.

4. Reminders & check-ins

  • Morning delivery — your tasks arrive automatically at your chosen hour
  • Check-ins through the day — ATLAS nudges you about unfinished tasks
  • Inactivity pings — vanish for a few days and ATLAS reaches out

7. Beta testers — your mission 🏆

The deal: use ATLAS for real for 3–4 weeks, answer 3 tiny questions each week, and earn lifetime ATLAS Pro — free, forever as a Founding Tester.

  • Set a real goal you actually care about — fake goals give fake feedback
  • Whenever something annoys, confuses, breaks, or delights you → /feedback your thought right in the bot. Ten tiny feedbacks beat one essay.
  • Things we especially want you to poke: onboarding clarity · task quality · whether the chat feels supportive or naggy · streaks · weekly review usefulness
  • Founding status is earned by activity — the logs decide, not the group membership 😉
Beta note: during beta, everything is free — every feature unlocked, no payments, no limits. That's the whole point: break it, stretch it, tell us everything.
