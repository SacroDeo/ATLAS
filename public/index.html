how it works

Three steps. Then it runs your day.

STEP 1 — once

Tell it the goal

"Crack SOC analyst interviews in 3 months." ATLAS interviews you about your time, level, and struggles, then builds a phased roadmap that fits your life — not a template.

STEP 2 — every morning

Get today's missions

Wake up to 3–4 concrete tasks generated from your current roadmap phase. Sized to your schedule, with the why attached. Tap ✅ when done.

STEP 3 — every week

See it come together

Weekly reviews show what moved and what stalled, streaks keep your momentum visible, and the roadmap advances phase by phase as the work gets done.
