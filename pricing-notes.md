## Principles (settled across many discussions — the "why")
1. Never meter conversation or daily tasks. No chat caps, no task caps — metering the habit kills the habit.
2. Free = complete habit loop for ONE goal, forever good. It's the growth engine.
3. Pro = wider (more goals) + deeper (memory, intelligence, analytics) — "ATLAS becomes my personal coach," not "limits removed."
4. Sell outcomes ("it knows me, it adapts, it catches me before I fail") — never architecture.
